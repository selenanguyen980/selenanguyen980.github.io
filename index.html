Attribution Model Revamp

Co-led, with another junior BI analyst, a rebuild of the last-touch attribution logic that credits pipeline to Marketing, SDR, and Sales. Opportunities missing a qualifying meeting or recent activity used to fall out of the model as “unattributable.” We added fallback rules that credit the person who created the record, so every deal now has an owner. I presented the before-and-after impact to Revenue Operations leadership.

Business Impact Unattributed weighted pipeline: 34% → 0% · Credited deals up 20%+ for both Sales and SDR

Renewal Ownership Analysis

Built a weekly snapshot of open renewal accounts that tracks account tier and owner changes week over week, using point-in-time joins on versioned account and opportunity history. Then measured the gap between an account-owner change and the renewal date, grouped into 10-day buckets, and compared Closed Won with Closed Lost renewals along with the revenue at stake.
